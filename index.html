Bandhavgarh National Park

India's Highest Tiger Density Reserve

Set in Umaria surrounding an ancient hill fort, Bandhavgarh holds one of India's most famous wild Bengal tiger populations. Operating October to June, Vyom Travels organizes safari permits, gypsy slots, certified naturalists, and seamless car transfers from Indore & Jabalpur.
