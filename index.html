Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about outstation car rentals, pricing inclusions, and safari permit rules.

Are fuel, driver allowance, and vehicle charges included in the estimated fare?
▼
Yes! All daily rates listed on Vyom Travels include car rental, fuel, driver daily allowance (Bhatta), and vehicle insurance. State entry taxes, toll plaza taxes, and parking fees (if applicable) are paid directly as per actual highway receipts.
How do I book Bandhavgarh Tiger Safari permits?
▼
You can select your preferred core gate (Tala, Magadhi, Khitauli) or buffer zone, enter travel dates, and submit your inquiry via our WhatsApp popup. Aakash Jaiswal will verify official MP Forest Dept slot availability and handle permit issuance. Original Government photo IDs are required for all safari passengers.
Can I book a same-day return cab from Indore to Ujjain Mahakal Corridor?
▼
Absolutely. Same-day return trips for Ujjain Bhasma Aarti and Mahakal Lok temple darshan are very popular. Our chauffeurs know the best parking zones near temple gates to save you walking time.
What are the cancellation and modification policies?
▼
Vehicle rentals cancelled at least 24 hours before pickup receive 100% advance refund. Bandhavgarh tiger safari permits are non-transferable as per Madhya Pradesh Forest Department regulations.
